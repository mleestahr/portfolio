When You Give a Cat a Lemon | Short Animated Film

Together, Calleigh Hoang and I created this short film in forty-eight hours for the Slug48 Hour Film Competition in April 2025. The film won "Best Use of Required Dialogue" and went on to be selected for the Mill Valley Film Festival in October 2026.
