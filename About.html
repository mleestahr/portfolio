ABOUT

I am a multimedia artist from the San Francisco Bay Area, currently pursuing a Masters of Design with a specialization in Animation at San Jose State University.

I hold a BA from the University of California, Santa Cruz in Film and Digital Media, where I had the honor to explore live action narrative and documentary filmmaking.

I specialize in illustration, animation, and dance videography, and have always been drawn to how art can be used to tell complex and emotionally nuanced stories. I’m interested in how -- in an era of artificial intelligence and political turmoil -- art can shed light on the collective human condition. What differentiates a living person from a machine? Why is it even more important to highlight a myriad of unique experiences in the media today? I also am propelled to use my artistic practice to process complex emotions, make sense of hierarchical social structures, and to reflect the world in which we live in.
